# Spike Editor links

- [Screen 1 — Spike Editor (Google Sites)](https://sites.google.com/view/spikeeditor/home)
- [Screen 2 — Spike (GitHub Pages)](https://edking21.github.io/Spike-Editor/index.html)

Ctrl+clicking a link above opens it in your regular browser. That does not necessarily share the tab with Copilot.

To let Copilot inspect or click the page, ask it in chat to open the URL in its shared browser, for example:

> Open Screen 1 and Screen 2 in the shared browser. Compare what happens when I click the Getting Started buttons.

Once the pages appear as shared browser pages in the chat, Copilot can inspect their screens, click controls, and help debug behavior such as clipboard copying.
